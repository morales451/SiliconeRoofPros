Why Austin Businesses Choose Silicone Roof Pros

Central Texas runs a flat roof through two extremes. Roughly 300 sunny days a year bake the membrane, then a single storm drops several inches of rain in a couple of hours. The sun does the slow damage; the rain finds every seam that damage opened.

A white silicone surface answers both halves of that. It reflects up to 85% of the sunlight doing the slow damage, and it cures into one continuous membrane with no seams for the downpour to exploit. Energy Star rated, so it counts toward your building's efficiency targets as well as its roof budget.

From the tech campuses at the Domain to the shopfronts on South Congress, downtown high-rises to the warehouses along I-35, we work Austin roofs of every size. And when a roof is too far gone to coat, we say so rather than sell you a membrane over wet insulation.

Austin's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle Central Texas's toughest conditions

Intense Sun & Heat

Around 300 sunny days a year, with summer highs well past 100°F. A dark roof can reach 160°F; coated white it runs up to 50°F cooler, which slows membrane ageing and cuts what your AC has to remove.

Flash Flooding

Central Texas storms arrive fast and heavy, and flat roofs cannot drain that quickly. Water sits in the low spots for days afterwards. Silicone is unaffected by standing water; acrylic softens and slowly re-emulsifies in it.

UV Degradation

Austin gets more clear-sky days and far less coastal cloud cover than the Gulf, so roofs here take more direct UV hours per year. Silicone is inherently UV-stable and will not chalk, crack or degrade the way acrylic does under that exposure.

Sustainability Goals

Coating a roof instead of replacing it keeps tons of old membrane out of landfill and cuts the building's cooling load for two decades. The system is Energy Star rated, which helps with green building targets — and some local efficiency rebates, worth checking before you start.

Commercial Roof Services in Austin

Complete flat roof restoration solutions for Austin businesses

TPO Roof Coating

TPO ages fastest at the laps and fastener plates. We seal both under one membrane and add 20+ years to the roof you have.

EPDM Roof Restoration

Aging EPDM goes brittle and pulls at the flashings. We bridge every seam with one continuous silicone membrane.

Metal Roof Coating

Metal panels expand and contract all day in Central Texas sun. Silicone seals rust, leaks and thermal movement together.

Cool Roof Installation

An Energy Star rated white finish that runs up to 50°F cooler than a dark roof and cuts summer cooling costs by as much as 35%.

Modified Bitumen Coating

Once the granules go, Central Texas sun attacks the asphalt directly. Silicone re-covers it and adds decades of life.
