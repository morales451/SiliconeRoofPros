How Commercial Roof Restoration Saves You Thousands in Repairs

Back to Blog

Here is the part that surprises building owners: on most aging commercial roofs, the roof is fine. The deck is sound, the insulation is dry, and what has actually failed is the seams, fasteners and flashings — the joints between the pieces. Tearing all of it off to fix the joints is how a $270,000 problem becomes a $520,000 one. Here is the arithmetic.

The Math Behind the Savings

Run the numbers on a typical 40,000 sq ft commercial building, using the same per-square-foot bands we quote from:

Cost Comparison: 40,000 sq ft Building

Full Roof Replacement $320,000 - $480,000
Silicone Roof Restoration $120,000 - $160,000
Your Potential Savings $200,000 - $320,000

Where the Savings Come From

1. No Tear-Off Costs

Tearing a roof off is labour, equipment and landfill fees — all spent before a single square foot of new roof goes down. Restoration coats straight over what is there, so that entire line disappears from the quote.

2. Reduced Material Costs

A full roof system includes decking, insulation, membranes, and accessories. Restoration requires only the coating material and any necessary repairs to the existing system.

3. Faster Installation

Roof replacement can take weeks, accumulating labor costs each day. Restoration is typically completed in days, dramatically reducing labor expenses.

4. No Business Disruption

This is the cost nobody puts on the quote. A tear-off means weeks of demolition noise over your tenants, debris in the parking lot, and a building that is open to the weather every night. Retail tenants lose trade. Warehouses lose picking hours. Some owners end up abating rent. Restoration is quiet, contained, and never leaves your building open.

The Tax Advantage You Can't Ignore

Here's where restoration becomes even more attractive:

  • Roof Replacement: Classified as a capital improvement, depreciated over 39 years
  • Roof Restoration: Classified as maintenance, 100% deductible in year one

A $150,000 restoration deducted in full at a 35% rate is about $52,500 back in year one. The same money spent on a replacement is depreciated instead, returning roughly $1,350 a year for thirty-nine years. Confirm your own treatment with your CPA — it depends on the scope of the work — but this timing difference is usually the biggest number in the comparison.

Long-Term Value

But doesn't a new roof last longer? Not necessarily. Consider this:

  • Average new commercial roof warranty: 10-20 years
  • Silicone restoration warranty: 20 years
  • Restoration can be reapplied, further extending life

With proper maintenance, a restored roof can outlast a new one while costing significantly less.

Additional Savings: Energy Efficiency

Reflective roof coatings provide ongoing savings through reduced cooling costs:

  • Reduce roof surface temperature by up to 50°F
  • Lower cooling costs by 20-35%
  • For our 40,000 sq ft example: $8,000-$15,000 annual savings

Across a 20-year warranty term, the energy saving alone can total $160,000–$300,000 — on a building where the coating itself cost a fraction of that. See how the cooling savings are calculated.

When Replacement Is Necessary

Restoration isn't always the answer. Full replacement may be needed when:

  • The roof deck is structurally compromised
  • Insulation is saturated with water throughout
  • More than 25% of the roof requires major repairs
  • Building codes require upgrade to current standards

An infrared scan settles it in one evening: wet insulation glows, dry insulation does not. That single test is what separates a roof worth coating from a roof worth replacing. See how the inspection works.
