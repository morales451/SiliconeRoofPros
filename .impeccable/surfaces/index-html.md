## Scope

Homepage (index.html), Persuade. The world it sets carries every page of the site.

## Audience and action

A commercial owner or property manager with a leaking or patched flat roof. Two equal actions: outline the roof on the satellite quote, or call the regional number. Proof is the real process, prices and the moisture-scan deliverable, never the unverified testimonials or logos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the roof-survey deliverable itself, not a contractor brochure. The visitor's own roof is the hero, framed as a survey viewport, and gets measured on the spot. It refuses the category default: a stock-photo hero with a blue gradient, a check-mark benefit list and rows of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: This is the moisture-survey sheet (assets/icons/aerial-scan.jpg).
- Grounds: a graphite orthophoto ground (#1b1d21), cool white report pages, and hairline survey rules.
- Marks: red diagonal hatching marks wet or failing areas. An iron-palette thermal scale bar (violet → magenta → orange → amber) is the signature strip.
- Action colour: amber.
- Type: Archivo, semi-condensed for display. Figures are tabular readouts (area, $/sq ft, mils, years).
- Shape: square corners, no shadows except the sheet lift, legend keys instead of icon tiles.

STORY: Your roof leaks at its seams, and the scan shows where. One membrane removes the seams. The numbers ($3.50–$5 against $8–$15, 3–7 days, 10/15/20 years) make the case. Outline your roof now, or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Left 5/12: the H1, one subhead and two actions (the address field, and the phone with its region).
- Right 7/12: a large graphite survey viewport holding the live map, with corner registration marks, a crosshair, a vertical thermal scale bar and a HUD readout (AREA ft² / EST $) that fills when the roof is outlined.
- The address input sits on the viewport's top edge as the survey's search bar.
- Signature: the outlined polygon drives the HUD readouts live.
- Motion: one scan-line sweep on load.

FORM: Thermal infrared / moisture scan imagery, #5 on the grounded list. Seed key dfa6acce, assigned direction.
- Raise (Nixie laboratory counter): every figure is set as an instrument readout, tabular and unit-labelled, never as a "big stat" hero metric.
- Raise (console dashboard): density courage. Readouts and legends sit packed in strips, not spread across airy cards.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
