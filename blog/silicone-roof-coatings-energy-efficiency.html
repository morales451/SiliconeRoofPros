How Silicone Roof Coatings Improve Energy Efficiency

Back to Blog

Traditional roofing materials absorb heat, causing increased HVAC expenses. Silicone roof coatings offer a revolutionary solution that can reduce your energy costs by up to 35% through their highly reflective properties.

The Science Behind Cool Roofing

Dark-colored roofs can reach temperatures of 150°F or more on a hot summer day. This heat transfers into your building, forcing your air conditioning system to work overtime. Silicone roof coatings, typically white or light-colored, reflect up to 85% of the sun's rays back into the atmosphere.

This reflectivity is measured by two key metrics:

  • Solar Reflectance Index (SRI): Silicone coatings typically achieve an SRI of 100 or higher, compared to 5-20 for traditional dark roofs.
  • Thermal Emittance: The ability to release absorbed heat. Silicone coatings excel here, preventing heat buildup.

Real-World Energy Savings

Studies have shown that cool roof coatings can reduce peak cooling demand by 10-15% and lower annual air conditioning energy use by 20-35%. For a 50,000 square foot commercial building in Houston, this can translate to savings of $15,000-$25,000 per year in energy costs alone.

Additional Benefits Beyond Energy Savings

While energy efficiency is a major advantage, silicone roof coatings provide several other benefits:

  • Extended Roof Life: By reducing thermal shock (the expansion and contraction from temperature changes), silicone coatings can extend your roof's lifespan by 15-20 years.
  • Waterproofing: Silicone is naturally waterproof and won't chalk, crack, or peel over time.
  • UV Protection: The reflective properties also protect the underlying roof membrane from UV degradation.
  • Reduced Urban Heat Island Effect: Cool roofs help lower ambient temperatures in urban areas.

Is Silicone Coating Right for Your Building?

Silicone roof coatings work best on flat or low-slope roofs, which are common in commercial buildings. They can be applied over most existing roof types, including:

  • Modified bitumen
  • Built-up roofing (BUR)
  • Single-ply membranes (TPO, EPDM, PVC)
  • Metal roofs
  • Spray polyurethane foam (SPF)

Tax Benefits and Incentives

Roof restoration with silicone coatings is 100% tax-deductible as a maintenance expense under IRS guidelines. Additionally, many utility companies offer rebates for cool roof installations, and your building may qualify for LEED credits.
