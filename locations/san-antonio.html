Why San Antonio Businesses Choose Silicone Roof Pros

South Texas heat does not arrive in spells here, it settles in. San Antonio roofs run past 100°F for weeks at a stretch, which cooks the flexibility out of a membrane — and then a thunderstorm drops inches of rain in an afternoon onto whatever that heat has opened up.

A white silicone membrane attacks the root of that. Reflecting up to 85% of the sunlight keeps the roof up to 50°F cooler, so it ages far more slowly — and because the coating cures seamless, the storm that follows has nothing to get into.

San Antonio's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle South Texas's toughest conditions

Scorching Summers

Not a hot week — hot months. Sustained 100°F-plus days drive a dark roof to around 160°F. Coated white it runs up to 50°F cooler, which protects the membrane below and cuts cooling costs by as much as 35%.

Sudden Storms

Rain here arrives all at once. Flat roofs cannot drain at that rate, so water stands in the low spots for days. Silicone is unaffected by ponding; acrylic softens and slowly breaks down in it.

UV Degradation

UV is what actually ages a South Texas roof — it breaks down the binders holding the membrane together. Silicone is inherently UV-stable, so it will not chalk, crack or go brittle the way acrylic does under the same sun.

Military & Government

Military and government facilities here run on documented specifications and inspection records. We apply to manufacturer spec, log wet mil thickness as we go, and hand over the paperwork to prove it.
