Houston's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle Houston's toughest conditions

Extreme Heat

Houston summers regularly exceed 95°F with intense UV exposure. Our reflective coating reduces roof surface temperature by up to 50°F, protecting your roof membrane and slashing AC costs.

Heavy Rainfall

With 50+ inches of annual rainfall and sudden downpours, Houston roofs need superior waterproofing. Silicone creates a seamless, 100% waterproof barrier that handles ponding water without degradation.

Hurricane Season

Houston's Gulf Coast location means hurricane preparedness is essential. Our silicone coating adds an extra layer of protection, adhering firmly to your existing roof and resisting wind-driven rain.

Humidity & Moisture

Houston's high humidity accelerates roof deterioration. Silicone is inherently moisture-resistant and won't absorb water, preventing the blistering and bubbling common with other roof coatings.
