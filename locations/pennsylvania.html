Why Pennsylvania Building Owners Restore Instead of Re-Roof

Pennsylvania flat roofs fail in winter. Water works into a seam, freezes, expands and pries it a little wider. It thaws, runs deeper, and freezes again. Southeastern Pennsylvania goes through that cycle dozens of times every winter, on top of more than 40 inches of rain a year and nor'easters that drive water sideways. A roof that looked fine in October is leaking in three places by March.

Silicone breaks that cycle. It cures into one seamless membrane, so there are no laps for meltwater to work into, and it stays flexible far below freezing, so it moves with the deck instead of cracking. It is also the one fluid-applied coating unaffected by standing water — which matters when drains ice over and snowmelt sits on the roof for days.

Pennsylvania's Flat Roof Challenges

What a Mid-Atlantic winter does to a flat roof, and how silicone handles each one

Freeze-Thaw Cycles

Water that gets into a seam freezes, expands, and pries the seam open a little further every time. Silicone has no seams to pry, and it stays flexible well below freezing, so it moves with the roof instead of cracking.

Snowmelt & Ponding Water

When drains ice over, meltwater has nowhere to go and sits on the roof for days. Silicone is unaffected by standing water; acrylic coatings soften and slowly break down in it.

Nor'easters

Nor'easters push rain sideways, under laps and flashings that ordinary rain never reaches. A bonded, seamless membrane has no laps for the wind to drive water under — and no loose edges for it to peel.

Hot, Humid Summers

Philadelphia summers are hot and sticky. A white silicone surface reflects up to 85% of sunlight, so the building takes on less heat and your cooling bills drop. On most low-rise commercial buildings, that summer saving outweighs the small extra winter heating a reflective roof can cause.

Commercial Roof Services in Pennsylvania

Complete flat roof restoration for Philadelphia, its suburbs and the Lehigh Valley

TPO Roof Coating

Pennsylvania winters leave aging TPO stiff and its seams brittle. A silicone overlay seals every lap and adds 20+ years without a tear-off.

EPDM Roof Restoration

EPDM shrinks in the cold and pulls away from its flashings at the corners. Silicone bridges those seams with one flexible membrane.

Metal Roof Coating

Swinging from summer heat to winter freezes works the fasteners loose on metal roofs year after year. Silicone seals the panels, seams and screw heads as one.

Built-Up Roof Sealing

Plenty of older Philadelphia buildings still have built-up roofs. We restore them in place, without tearing down to the deck.

Modified Bitumen Coating

Snow, ice and shovels strip mod-bit granules and expose the asphalt underneath. A silicone coat re-covers it and adds decades of protection.

Winter Inspections, Spring Coating

Silicone goes down on a dry roof above freezing, so coating runs from spring through fall. Inspections and quotes run all year — book in winter to be first on the spring schedule.
