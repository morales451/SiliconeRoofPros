Dallas-Fort Worth's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle North Texas's toughest conditions

Extreme Heat

Dallas summers regularly exceed 100°F with intense UV exposure. Our reflective coating reduces roof surface temperature by up to 50°F, protecting your membrane and slashing AC costs significantly.

Hail Damage

DFW sits in "Hail Alley" with frequent severe storms. Silicone coating adds an extra protective layer and can be easily recoated if damaged, at a fraction of replacement cost.

Temperature Swings

DFW can see 40-degree temperature swings in a single day. Our flexible silicone expands and contracts with your roof, preventing cracks and maintaining a perfect seal year-round.

Severe Storms

DFW averages 40+ thunderstorms annually with heavy rain and high winds. Our seamless silicone membrane creates a 100% waterproof barrier that stands up to the most severe weather.
