Why Silicone Leads for Most Flat Roofs

Silicone is our most-recommended system — here's the honest reasoning

The Ponding Water Problem

Flat roofs collect water. Silicone is the only fluid-applied coating that actually gets stronger with water contact — every other system degrades over time.

Texas Heat Is Brutal

Silicone stays flexible in extreme UV and heat without cracking or shrinking. That matters when your roof sees 100°F+ summers every single year.

Longest Warranty Available

Silicone's durability is why manufacturers will back it with up to a 20-year warranty. No other fluid-applied coating type comes close.

Best Long-Term Value

Silicone costs more upfront than acrylic. But it lasts 2–3x longer, needs less maintenance, and avoids costly recoating down the road.
