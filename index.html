Why Flat Roofs Leak — And Why Patching Never Ends

A Flat Roof Doesn't Fail in the Middle. It Fails at the Seams.

A commercial flat roof is hundreds of separate pieces — membrane sheets, fasteners, flashings, curbs, drains — glued and screwed into one surface. Water pools. UV bakes the membrane. Screws back out and rust. Seams split. Every joint is somewhere a leak can start.

And a leak is never just a roof bill. It's ruined inventory, an angry tenant, and a claim against your deductible.

Most flat roof companies answer that with one more patch — the same method that just failed you.

Water Ponding & Algae Growth
Patches on Patches

Roof Repairs Are TEMPORARY

A flat roof repair holds for a while.

Then one rusty screw, one cracked joint, or one loose seam opens the next leak — usually ten feet from the last one.

Soon you're patching the patches. Any single visit looks cheap. Five years of them costs more than restoring the roof once — and at the end you still have the same old roof.

Roof Replacement Is COSTLY

Tear-off and replacement runs roughly $8 to $15 per square foot. On a 30,000 sq ft roof, that is a quarter of a million dollars — plus weeks of dumpsters, noise, and open decking every time rain is forecast.

And for that money you get the same design that just failed — hundreds of pieces sealed together, with all the same seams.

The new membrane starts ageing on day one. It thins, it shrinks, it cracks.

A re-roof doesn't remove the problem. It resets the clock on it — and the IRS makes you depreciate every dollar over 39 years.
