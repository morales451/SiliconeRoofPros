San Antonio's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle South Texas's toughest conditions

Scorching Summers

San Antonio summers regularly exceed 100°F for weeks at a time. Our reflective coating reduces roof surface temperature by up to 50°F, protecting your membrane and slashing AC costs by up to 35%.

Sudden Storms

South Texas is known for intense, sudden rainfall. Our seamless silicone membrane creates a 100% waterproof barrier that handles ponding water without degradation - essential for flat commercial roofs.

UV Degradation

San Antonio's intense sunlight accelerates roof aging. Silicone is inherently UV-stable and won't chalk, crack, or degrade like lesser coatings, maintaining performance for decades.

Military & Government

San Antonio's large military and government presence demands reliable, long-lasting roofing solutions. Our systems meet and exceed federal facility requirements.
