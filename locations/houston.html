Why Houston Businesses Trust Silicone Roof Pros

Houston gives a flat roof every problem at once: 100°F-plus summers, more than 50 inches of rain a year, Gulf humidity that never lets a seam dry out, and a hurricane season that tests all of it. Roofs here do not fail slowly. They fail the week of a storm.

Silicone is the right answer for that combination. It is the one fluid-applied coating unaffected by standing water, which matters when Houston roofs drain slowly and pond for days. The white surface also runs up to 50°F cooler than a dark membrane, so your AC works less all summer.

Houston's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle Houston's toughest conditions

Extreme Heat

A dark Houston roof can sit at 160°F in August. Coated white, the same roof runs up to 50°F cooler — which protects the membrane underneath and takes real load off your chillers.

Heavy Rainfall

Fifty-plus inches a year, much of it arriving in an afternoon. Flat Houston roofs drain slowly and hold water for days. Silicone is the only fluid-applied coating that is unaffected by that — acrylic softens and slowly re-emulsifies.

Hurricane Season

Wind-driven rain finds laps and flashings that ordinary rain never reaches. A bonded, seamless membrane has no laps to drive water under — and no loose edges for wind to catch and peel.

Humidity & Moisture

Gulf humidity keeps roofs damp long after the rain stops, and trapped moisture is what blisters and bubbles a coating. Silicone does not absorb water, so it does not blister — provided the roof is fully dry before we spray, which is why we wait for it.

Commercial Roof Services in Houston

Complete flat roof restoration solutions for Houston businesses

TPO Roof Coating

Houston TPO roofs chalk and shrink under Gulf sun until the seams pull apart. Coating them adds 20+ years without a tear-off.

EPDM Roof Restoration

Houston humidity works into EPDM seams and lifts them. A seamless silicone membrane bridges every lap and stops the leaks for good.

Metal Roof Coating

Coastal air rusts fasteners and seams on Houston metal roofs. Silicone seals the panels and stops the corrosion spreading.

Built-Up Roof Sealing

Restore aging BUR systems without the cost and disruption of full replacement.

Modified Bitumen Coating

Mod-bit granules wash off fast in 50 inches of annual rain. A UV-stable silicone coat adds decades to what is left.

Ponding Water Solutions

Unlike other coatings, silicone thrives in standing water - perfect for Houston's flat roofs.
