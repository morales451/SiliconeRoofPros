Roof Repairs Using Silicone, Acrylic, and Asphalt Repair Products

Back to Blog

Not all roof repair products are created equal. The right choice depends on your roof material, condition, climate, and budget. In this guide, we compare the pros and cons of silicone, acrylic, and asphalt repair solutions to help you make an informed decision.

Quick Comparison

Feature Silicone Acrylic Asphalt
Waterproofing Excellent Good Good
UV Resistance Excellent Good Poor
Ponding Water Excellent Poor Fair
Lifespan 15-20 years 10-15 years 5-10 years
Cost Higher Moderate Lower

Silicone Roof Coatings

Pros:

  • Superior waterproofing - silicone is inherently hydrophobic
  • Excellent ponding water resistance - won't break down in standing water
  • Outstanding UV resistance - maintains reflectivity for decades
  • Stays flexible in extreme temperatures
  • Longest lifespan of all coating options (15-20 years)
  • Single-coat application possible

Cons:

  • Higher upfront cost
  • Can attract dirt (though this doesn't affect performance)
  • Requires proper surface preparation

Best for: Flat roofs with ponding water issues, buildings in humid climates, long-term solutions.

Acrylic Roof Coatings

Pros:

  • Lower cost than silicone
  • Good reflectivity and energy savings
  • Easy to apply and recoat
  • Breathable - allows moisture vapor to escape
  • Maintains clean appearance longer

Cons:

  • Not suitable for ponding water - can re-emulsify
  • Requires multiple coats
  • Less flexible in cold temperatures
  • Shorter lifespan than silicone

Best for: Roofs with good drainage, dry climates, budget-conscious projects.

Asphalt-Based Products

Pros:

  • Lowest upfront cost
  • Good adhesion to existing asphalt roofs
  • Familiar to most contractors
  • Quick repairs possible

Cons:

  • Poor UV resistance - degrades in sunlight
  • Black color absorbs heat - no energy savings
  • Cracks and becomes brittle over time
  • Shortest lifespan
  • May require more frequent maintenance

Best for: Temporary repairs, matching existing asphalt systems, emergency patches.

Our Recommendation

For most commercial flat roofs in the Houston area, we recommend silicone roof coatings. While the initial investment is higher, the long-term value is unmatched. With a 20-year warranty, superior waterproofing, and significant energy savings, silicone provides the best return on investment.
