About Us

Your trusted partner in commercial roof restoration

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Silicone Roof Pros specializes in commercial fluid-applied roof restoration systems. With over 10 years of expertise in the coatings industry, our founder understood one fundamental truth: "The key to success is doing right by the customer."

We saw too many building owners and facility managers being told their only option was a costly full roof replacement. We knew there was a better way - one that could save them money while delivering the same protection and performance.

That's why we focus exclusively on roof restoration using advanced silicone coatings. Our mission is to empower building owners and facility directors with affordable roof maintenance solutions that protect their investments without breaking the bank.
