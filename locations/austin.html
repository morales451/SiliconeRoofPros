Austin's Flat Roof Challenges

Our silicone coating system is engineered to handle Central Texas's toughest conditions

Intense Sun & Heat

Austin averages 300 sunny days per year with summer temps regularly exceeding 100°F. Our reflective coating reduces roof surface temperature by up to 50°F, dramatically cutting your AC costs.

Flash Flooding

Austin is notorious for sudden, intense rainfall events. Our seamless silicone membrane creates a 100% waterproof barrier that handles ponding water without degradation - essential for flat roofs.

UV Degradation

High-altitude Central Texas means more intense UV exposure than coastal areas. Silicone is inherently UV-stable and won't chalk, crack, or degrade like acrylic coatings.

Sustainability Goals

Austin businesses lead in sustainability. Our Energy Star rated coating helps you meet green building goals, reduce carbon footprint, and potentially qualify for local rebates.
